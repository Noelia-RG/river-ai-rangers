What is River AI Rangers?
A free, open-source programme that turns science and geography lessons into live environmental investigations. No installation. No subscriptions. Free to use.
📊

Real data

Students work with actual water-quality readings from their local river group — pH, nitrates, phosphates, and more.

🤖

AI investigation

A purpose-built AI assistant helps students interpret the data and question its answers — building critical AI literacy.

✉️

Civic action

Every lesson ends with a real output — a letter, a briefing, a poster — directed at a real audience.

🔓

Fully free

MIT licence. All materials are open-source. Schools can adapt and redeploy everything at no cost.

How it works
Four steps, one browser, zero installation.
1

Upload river data

Teacher uploads a CSV from the local river monitoring group.

2

AI reads the data

The assistant loads the latest readings and is ready to answer questions in context.

3

Students investigate

Students question the AI, challenge its answers, and form their own conclusions.

4

Take action

Every session ends with a civic output directed at someone who can act on it.

Lessons
Fully scripted, curriculum-aligned lessons for KS3/KS4. All free to download and adapt.
Lesson 1 · 60 min

Meet the River

Read pH, nitrates and phosphates. Form a hypothesis. Question the AI. Write to a decision-maker.

Lesson 2 · 60 min

Follow the Pollution

Compare upstream vs downstream data. Build a causal argument. Brief a real stakeholder.

Lesson 3 · 60 min

The Oxygen Crisis

Investigate dissolved oxygen. Trace the cascade from nutrients to ecological collapse.
