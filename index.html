What is it?

Citizen science meets AI

A free Streamlit app that puts real Thames21 river data in students’ hands — with an AI tutor built in to help them interpret it.

Student
Why is the phosphate level so high at Site 3?
AI Ranger
Looking at the data, Site 3 shows 0.42 mg/L — that’s four times the healthy limit of 0.1 mg/L. This often points to agricultural run-off or a nearby sewage overflow. What land use is upstream of that site?
Student
There are farms. So is that illegal?
AI Ranger
Great question — and one worth checking. The Environment Agency sets legal limits, but enforcement is patchy. Here’s how to find out who’s responsible…

How it works

Up and running in three steps

No setup. No accounts. No data leaving the classroom without your knowledge.

1
📂

Load a dataset

Choose a preloaded Thames21 river dataset or upload your own CSV. The app reads the columns and prepares the AI automatically.

2
💬

Ask questions

Type a question or click a prompt card. The AI responds based on the real data in front of it — phosphates, ammonia, pH, dissolved oxygen.

3
🔍

Investigate & question

Push back, dig deeper, compare sites across seasons, and use the data to take action — write a report, a letter, or a class presentation.

Features

Built for the classroom

🆓

Completely free

No subscription, no per-student licence, no hidden cost. The app is open-source and free to use for any school.

🔒

Safe by design

The AI is instructed to redirect off-topic questions, never discuss personal topics, and remind students it can make mistakes.

📈

Health panel

Instant visual summary of average and peak readings for pH, phosphates, ammonia, nitrates and dissolved oxygen — with healthy-range flags.

📤

Upload your own data

If your school has conducted its own river monitoring, upload the CSV and the AI analyses your data — up to 5 MB.

🃏

Prompt cards

20 ready-made question starters across five categories: The Numbers, Compare Sites, Pollution, Take Action, and Question the AI.

⚡

Fast responses

Powered by Claude Haiku, responses arrive in seconds. A 30-question per session limit keeps API costs manageable for free use.

🌐

No install needed

Runs entirely in the browser. Students just need the link. Works on school laptops, tablets, even phones.

🔓

Open source

Fork it, modify it, run your own instance with your own API key. The code, data, and lesson materials are all on GitHub.
